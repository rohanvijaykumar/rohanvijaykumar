### About

With expertise in media and entertainment, AI, software and audio industries, I take messy, multi-team work from plan to delivery, and I build AI products along the way.

I spent three years at Deluxe Media steering 250+ film and TV titles through QC, localization and delivery, then ran operations at Northeastern University's Recording Studios while finishing an MS in Project Management. My first degree is in Audio Engineering. Today I'm a Project Manager at Humanitarians AI, and I'm building **Mez**.
